Autonomy Levels · 5 stages
Auto Acts autonomously. Reports after.
Notify Notifies while acting. Reversible.
Confirm Requires approval before acting.
Suggest Proposes only. Decision belongs to others.
Override Final authority to override any decision.
Selected Cell row 3, col 3
Dev × Tech Stack = Override
Dev holds final authority on architecture. He can reject proposals from the PO or agents — including an agent's "optimal implementation" recommendation. Any suggestion can be held or sent back.
Why Override, not Auto
Technical debt is a long-term commitment only humans can judge. An agent cannot reason about maintainability ten years from now.
Design Principle
This matrix is a governance design tool for the agentic era — it makes explicit who holds final authority in each domain. As more agents join the team, human roles shift from decision-makers to accountable owners.
