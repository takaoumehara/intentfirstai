Yamanaka Onsen — what to know

Family-run inn. No PMS, no in-room TV, no Wi-Fi past the lobby. The bath is shared by gender, but a small private family bath is bookable at the front desk for parents-with-kids — first-come from 18:30. Tattoos are not a concern for any of you.

1
Slippers off at the genkan
Switch to the inn's slippers waiting at the threshold. Shoes go on the rack to the right.
2
Yukata on, sash to the left
Right side first, then left over it. Sash tied at the side, not centered.
3
Small towel to the bath, big towel left in the changing room
Leave wallet, watch, phone in the locker. The small towel is for modesty, never enters the water.
4
Sit and rinse first — fully — before the bath
Stools and hand-showers are along the wall. Soap, scrub, rinse twice. Only then enter the water.
5
Soak slowly · 8–10 minutes max first time
42°C is hot for a Brooklyn body. Step out, sit, drink the water at the wall, return if you want.
Family bath Reserve at the front desk when you check in. 30-minute slot for the four of you. ¥2,200.
