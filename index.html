AI UX Design · Context Grammar

From human expression to agentic action.

Context Grammar is a decision pipeline for designing the space between what people mean and what AI does. It interprets intent, reads six situation signals, respects two relationship dials, passes risky actions through a Negotiation Gate, and resolves how much autonomy AI should have before it responds.

01 · Problem

Software is still app-first.

Every app decides its own layout, its own priority, its own rhythm — regardless of who you are or what moment you're in. AI agents are changing what software can do, but most interfaces still ask people to navigate menus and screens.

02 · Shift

Intent is the entry point. Interpretation is the interface.

Raw human expression rarely arrives as a clean command. The system interprets it across detection channels and awareness depth, then negotiates the hypothesis with the user before acting.
