Context Grammar — Stage 3

Dials

Trust is not one permission screen. One dial controls what the AI may know. The other controls what it may do.

01 Disclosure is information access, set per person and per domain.
02 Autonomy is decision authority, earned through evidence.
03 Autonomy cannot rise above the Disclosure that supports it.

Quick overview

Read this page as two product questions.

01

What can the AI know?

Disclosure has levels such as Full, Summary, Existence, and Hidden. It is not a yes/no privacy toggle.

02

What can the AI do?

Autonomy moves through Suggest, Confirm, Notify, and Auto. Each level is a different contract.

03

What order is valid?

Disclosure comes first. A system cannot responsibly act in a domain it is not allowed to understand.

04

Whose relationship?

A shared home AI still needs separate dial pairs for Mai, Kiran, Aoi, Sota, Leo, and every domain.

Restaurant model

Trust grows like becoming a regular.

The first time you visit a restaurant, you order carefully. After a few good visits, the waiter remembers what you like. Much later, you can say "surprise me" and still feel safe.

That progress requires two things at once: you reveal more about yourself, and the restaurant earns more authority to choose for you. One without the other is not trust.

Disclosure and Autonomy are those two movements. Context Grammar gives them names, levels, and rules so AI relationships can grow deliberately instead of by accident.

The distinction

An open window is not the same as handing over the steering wheel.

Disclosure Dial

How much does the AI know?

Information access: health, finance, family, location, calendar, or taste. The level can vary by domain and by person.

Autonomy Dial

How much can the AI act?

Decision authority: whether the AI only suggests, asks before execution, notifies after execution, or acts silently inside a trusted boundary.

Disclosure Dial

Disclosure has two axes: protect and connect.

Protect

Human → AI

What data do you let the AI see? Full, Summary, Existence, or Hidden. Health can be Full for a medical AI and Hidden from a hobby AI.

Connect

AI → Human

What may the AI mention to another person? Push, Digest, Available, or Off. Knowing Aoi's music is not the same as telling Kiran.

Sync

AI ↔ AI / external

What may be shared with other systems? A medical portal may receive Full, a parent may receive Summary, and school may receive Hidden.

Level Protect: AI may know Connect: AI may share Example
Full / Push Raw detail is visible. New information can be surfaced immediately. Medical AI sees complete medication history; daughter's art posts notify family immediately.
Summary / Digest Only an aggregate or label is visible. Updates are compressed into periodic summaries. Fitness AI sees "somewhat inactive"; dance videos become a weekly highlight.
Existence / Available The AI knows a domain exists, but not its details. The information is available only when asked. Travel AI knows dietary needs exist; Dad's Kindle can be browsed on request.
Hidden / Off The domain is not visible. The domain is not shared. Hobby AI sees no health data; private playlists stay private.
Share Disclosure V2: knowing is not telling

Aoi lets the family AI know her music taste. Sota can hear everything, Mai can hear a vague acknowledgment, and Kiran hears nothing. Intake Disclosure controls what AI knows. Share Disclosure controls who AI may tell. The V1 schema reserves disclosure.share_with; V2 enforces it.

Logical dependency

Disclosure must come first.

Most AI products start with automation levels and permissions. Context Grammar starts one step earlier: you cannot delegate what you have not disclosed.

Saying "surprise me" before mentioning a nut allergy is dangerous. Letting a finance AI act without knowing your balance or spending limits is the same design error.

The reverse is coherent: Full Disclosure with low Autonomy. A health tracker may see everything while still only suggesting. Disclosure sets the ceiling for Autonomy.

Invalid

Low Disclosure × High Autonomy

The AI is acting without the context required to act well. This is not just risky; it is logically incoherent.

Valid path

Open Disclosure, then raise Autonomy

Domains with enough Disclosure can accept delegation. Domains with no Disclosure remain at Suggest or ask for more context.

Autonomy Dial

Four stages, not a continuous slider.

Stage 01

Suggest

AI presents options. The human decides and executes. New domains always start here.

Stage 02

Confirm

AI narrows to one candidate and asks before execution. One tap or voice confirmation moves it forward.

Stage 03

Notify

AI acts first, then reports. The human can undo, audit, and change future rules.

Stage 04

Auto

AI acts silently inside a bounded routine. The log remains available and exceptions still break through.

Stage Yamashiro family example Restaurant metaphor
Suggest "Here are three soccer shoe options for Sota. Which do you want?" First visit: you read the menu and choose yourself.
Confirm "Piano school A - book the trial visit?" The waiter recommends sea bass and waits for your nod.
Notify "Aoi's club fee transferred. Tap to undo." The waiter pairs wine and mentions it while pouring.
Auto Weekly groceries reorder after 15 stable weeks. You sit down and a perfect meal appears. This took years.

Three forces

The user does not control Autonomy alone.

The common assumption is "I set my automation level." In practice, the Autonomy Dial is the product of three forces acting simultaneously.

Force 01

Service default

Netflix autoplay, Amazon 1-Click, Spotify auto-advance: shipped at a high Autonomy level by product decision.

Force 02

AI adjustment

The system promotes or demotes based on track record, confidence, and failure history.

Force 03

User override

"Stop asking me every time" and "always check this one" remain direct human interventions.

Concrete walkthrough

Mai's Tuesday morning.

"Sota's soccer shoes are too small. And Aoi's club fee - 8,500 yen - deadline is today."

AI reads Disclosure settings first

Sota's shoe size is Full for the family AI. Household finance is Full for Mai and Kiran. Aoi's school details are Summary: parents see category, not content.

Autonomy differs by domain

Children's essentials under 10,000 yen are Auto. Recurring monthly transfers are Notify. First-time enrollment actions are Confirm.

Three actions, three stages

Sota's shoes order silently. Aoi's club fee transfers and notifies. Piano school waits for Mai to confirm the trial booking.

UI reflects the contract

One routine action disappears, one action becomes a receipt, and one action becomes a confirmation request. The interface is generated by the dial pair.

Aoi's Disclosure runs in parallel

Aoi's own setting lets parents see that a club fee exists, not the details. Same transaction, two privacy layers, both honored.

Multi-Brain context

Dial pairs are per person and per domain.

The Yamashiro family shares one home AI, but there is no single shared Autonomy level or Disclosure setting. Every person x every domain has its own relationship contract.

The Coordinator Brain reads those dial pairs before deciding which agents should act and in what mode. An agent does not grant itself permission; the Coordinator checks on its behalf.

Q&A

Common questions hiring readers ask.

Is Disclosure just a privacy setting?

No. Privacy settings are usually binary. Disclosure has levels, is scoped by domain and person, and includes what AI may say to others.

Does Auto remove human involvement?

No. Auto removes per-action interruption inside a bounded routine. Logs, undo, exceptions, and demotion remain part of the contract.

Can Disclosure be closed later?

Yes. Closing the dial stops new information flow. Erasing what the Brain already learned is a separate forget operation.

What connects this to Signals & Dials and Brain?

Situation Signals read the observed situation, and Relationship Dials (Autonomy / Disclosure) define the relationship boundaries. Context Brain stores the memory and permissions, while Trust Design governs how much that memory can be accessed and how much action may follow.

Memory without trust controls becomes surveillance.
