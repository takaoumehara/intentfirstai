Honest about detection

Not all 6 Situation Signals are equally detectable today. Form Factor and Physical State are reliable (device API, activity recognition). Priority Weight and Cognitive Load are estimated (calendar density, time of day, recent activity). Social Exposure is inferred from weak signals. The two Dials (Autonomy, Disclosure) are user-configured, not detected.

Every Signal and Dial carries a Decision Schema record — confidence, source, and fallback_behavior — so the Negotiation Gate (Stage 5) can evaluate whether to proceed, preview, or ask. The spec defines the language. Detection confidence depends on each platform — and improves toward 2028.

    Negotiation Gate · Stage 5
    
    
    Gate behavior in this story
    
    
    
      Each moment in the Yamashiro Tuesday passes through the Negotiation Gate before any action is
      taken. The Gate evaluates Confidence × Risk × Reversibility × Sensitivity and outputs a gate
      decision. Three representative examples from this story:
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    07:15 — Mai's morning coffee
    
        
    
          High confidence (kitchen routine, calendar clear), low risk, fully reversible.
          Gate proceeds quietly without interruption.
          R39 · Proceed quietly (confidence ≥ 0.8, risk = LOW)
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    17:30 — Family arrives home
    
        
    
          Medium confidence on dinner intent (4 people, different preferences, no explicit request).
          Gate surfaces an Interpretation Preview before acting on the inferred meal plan.
          R38 · Interpretation Preview (0.4 ≤ confidence < 0.8)
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    22:00 — Sota's late snack pattern
    
        
    
          Health domain, child user, sensitivity = HIGH. Gate requires at least lightweight
          confirmation even when confidence is high — sensitivity overrides.
          R35 · Require confirmation (sensitivity = HIGH)
