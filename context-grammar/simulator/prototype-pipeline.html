AX Patterns · what fires

A1 Form Factor Transform phone → TV cast
D5 Substitution Modes milk-out → flexible
A3 Social-Aware Filtering but: intent overrides
D3 Proactive Nudge low load · gentle suggest
The intent test: A naive system would fire A3 Social-Aware Filtering and blur prices because "kids present." The framework also reads Intent = joint family decision. Joint decisions need price to be the conversation. So A3 is overruled by the intent layer. Prices stay visible.
