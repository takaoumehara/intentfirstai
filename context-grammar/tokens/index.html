Context Grammar — Stages 2–3

Signals & Dials

Stages 2 and 3 of the Decision Pipeline. Six Situation Signals read the current context. Two Relationship Dials capture what the user has established about trust and transparency. Each Signal and Dial is a Decision Schema record — not a sensor claim.

6 Situation Signals describe what is happening now.
2 Relationship Dials decide what AI may know and do.
1 The Rule Engine reads them together before acting.

Context Grammar is a decision schema, not a sensor spec.

Every Signal and Dial carries the same shape. This is what makes the framework inspectable, adjustable, and honest about uncertainty.

signal_name:
  value: ...
  source: explicit_user_input | system_state | behavioral_inference
          | sensor_signal | organizational_data | memory_learned
  confidence: 0.0–1.0
  user_confirmed: boolean
  fallback_behavior: "description of what AI does without this signal"
  implementation_readiness: high | mid | low

Context Grammar assumes partial signals, uncertain inference, user correction, and graceful fallback. No signal requires guaranteed sensor access.

What Situation Signals Do

AI needs a way to read the room.

Before AI chooses what to say or do, it needs to understand the situation it is speaking into. Situation signals make that situation explicit.

A skilled waiter reads many signals before speaking. A crowded table means low attention. A candlelit dinner changes what should be said aloud. A guest checking their watch makes time more important than menu detail. A narrow counter limits what can be served.

Context Grammar turns that instinct into six Situation Signals (Physical State, Cognitive Load, Social Exposure, Priority Weight, Form Factor, Feasibility) and two Relationship Dials (Autonomy Dial, Disclosure Dial).

Two Separate Concepts

6 Situation Signals & 2 Relationship Dials.

01

Situation Signals

Physical State, Cognitive Load, Social Exposure, Priority Weight, Form Factor, and Feasibility describe what is happening right now.

02

Relationship Dials

Autonomy and Disclosure Relationship Dials define the distance between person and AI: what the system may know, and how far it may act.

03

Brain supplies memory

Signals draw from Identity, Learning, and Now layers. They are live readings over structured memory, not a flat preference file.

04

Rule Engine decides

The Rule Engine combines Signals, Dials, Brain memory, Trust Design, and AX Patterns to decide whether to show, suppress, ask, or act.

Per-Signal Deep Dive

Definitions, mechanisms, rules, and forbidden patterns.

1. Physical State · "Are they rushing or relaxed?"
Mechanism Activity Recognition APIs classify walking, running, driving, and stillness. Watches add heart rate, stress, and sleep signals. The system compresses these into delivery choices: show, suppress, speak, or wait.
Signal sources Now Layer: accelerometer, heart rate, gyroscope, CarPlay or Android Auto state. Learning Layer: repeated commute sprint pattern. Identity Layer: chronic conditions or physical constraints.
Design rule When the state is unreadable, default to caution. Running or driving means voice-only, minimal, or suppressed. Change the form first, then the content.
Forbidden Sending long notifications to someone running, requiring screen interaction while driving, or treating delivered as read.
2. Cognitive Load · "How much bandwidth remains?"
Mechanism Time of day, calendar density, recent app switching, task completions, and behavioral history produce low, medium, high, or overload estimates. This Signal must carry uncertainty metadata.
Signal sources Now Layer: calendar fill and recent activity. Learning Layer: "weekday mornings 7:00-7:30 are overload" plus override history such as "No, I'm free."
Design rule High or overload means one screen, one decision. Exploration belongs in low-load moments, and users need an easy override.
Forbidden Claiming load as measured fact, showing multi-choice dialogs during overload, or omitting a graceful override.
3. Social Exposure · "Who else is watching?"
Mechanism Bluetooth and Wi-Fi proximity detect nearby devices. Voice ID distinguishes family members. Mirroring state comes from the OS. On-device vision can estimate headcount where available.
Signal sources Now Layer: nearby devices, Voice ID, mirroring, and screen share. Identity Layer: the relationship map that labels family, colleague, friend, or public.
Design rule Audience changes the filter. Colleagues present means suppress household finance. Child present means hide surprise planning. This Signal works with Disclosure Dial settings.
Forbidden Displaying amounts, passwords, or health data while screen-sharing, or broadcasting personal notifications on a family device.
4. Priority Weight · "What wins in this collision?"
Mechanism Layer 1 is current urgency from Now: deadline, notification type, time remaining. Layer 2 is learned tradeoff history: "weekday time beats meal quality." The Rule Engine multiplies both.
Signal sources Now Layer: urgency, calendar, budget, deadlines. Learning Layer: past collision decisions. Identity Layer: non-negotiable principles such as safety or medical constraints.
Design rule Make the tradeoff explicit when it matters: "I chose time over meal variety because pickup is in 20 minutes."
Forbidden Treating priority as a static ranked list, ignoring past corrections, or optimizing for the product's goal instead of the user's current collision.
5. Form Factor · "Which surface can carry this?"
Mechanism The OS already knows device state, Continuity, DeX, CarPlay, Cast, screen size, and input mode. AI selects the best surface for each content type.
Signal sources Now Layer: connected devices, screen size, input modality, car or AR state. Identity Layer: owned devices and primary surfaces.
Design rule Surface changes information density. Watch equals one line. Phone equals paragraph. TV equals columns. Voice equals one question at a time.
Forbidden Shrinking phone UI onto a watch, reading long text aloud, or treating responsive web as enough for ambient and voice contexts.
6. Feasibility · "What is actually possible?"
Mechanism Domain APIs supply inventory, delivery ETA, weather, store hours, account balance, and human availability. If blocked, Substitution Mode proposes alternatives.
Signal sources Now Layer: inventory, weather, ETA, store hours, balance. Learning Layer: what the user chose when the usual item was unavailable.
Substitution modes Exact, Flexible, Exploring, and Surprise govern how much latitude AI has when the original request is blocked.
Design rule Never end at "impossible." Attach one to three alternatives. Feasibility and Substitution Mode should ship together.
Forbidden Ending with "out of stock" and nothing more, saying "doable" after checking one constraint, or substituting in Exact mode without disclosure.
7. Autonomy Dial · "How far can AI act?"
Stages Suggest: show options. Confirm: propose one action and ask before execution. Notify: execute and report after. Auto: execute silently.
Mechanism Three forces determine the dial: service default, AI adjustment, and user choice. Each domain, such as finance, health, work, or parenting, needs its own dial.
Signal sources Now Layer: transaction size, reversibility, urgency. Learning Layer: success rate and user intervention. Identity Layer: "never automate" declarations.
Design rule Start at Suggest. Advance one stage at a time as track record improves. Drop a stage after failure. Never-automate domains stay locked.
Forbidden One global automation toggle, Auto on first use, high autonomy after failure, or raised Autonomy while Disclosure is Hidden.
8. Disclosure Dial · "How much does AI know?"
Levels Full: all data. Summary: aggregates only. Existence: the domain exists, details hidden. Hidden: AI does not know this domain exists.
Mechanism Domain such as finances, health, work, parenting, relationships, or hobbies multiplied by recipient such as AI, family, colleagues, friends, or public creates a matrix.
Signal sources Identity Layer: declarative settings. Learning Layer: past manual privatization history such as "she hid this kind of content before."
Design rule Hidden domains cannot be set to Auto. You cannot delegate what you have not disclosed. Ask for Disclosure before increasing Autonomy.
Forbidden Reducing privacy to ON/OFF, allowing Hidden × Auto, or using data for learning without explicit consent.

How Signals and Dials Combine

No single Signal or Dial decides behavior. The read comes from the combination.

Combo 1 · 07:02 fridge

"What should I put in the bento?"

Cognitive Load is overload, Physical State is standing with both hands occupied, Social Exposure is family-only, and Autonomy for morning food is Auto-suggest. Decision: show one line, no tap required.

Combo 2 · Meeting room

"Surface the tutoring-cost discussion?"

Social Exposure is colleagues, Form Factor is a mirrored TV, Feasibility says the discussion can wait, Disclosure hides household finances from colleagues, and Autonomy requires Notify. Decision: suppress and resurface at 19:30.

Signals × Brain

Signals feed the Now Layer, Dials define the contract.

Every Brain has three Layers: Identity for what rarely changes, Learning for what accumulates, and Now for what is in flux. The 6 Situation Signals represent the active situation, feeding directly into the Brain's Now Layer.

Priority Weight is the clearest example: current urgency from Now, learned tradeoffs from Learning, and non-negotiable constraints from Identity. Physical State draws mainly from Now. Relationship Dials (Autonomy and Disclosure) map human-AI relationship boundaries rather than describing the observed situation.

Brain stores memory. Situation signals capture the moment. Relationship Dials define relationship limits. Rule Engine makes the judgment. A good explanation should point to how they combine to decide the final action.

Establishing Relationship Dials

Disclosure became its own Dial to build a complete relationship contract.

Q&A

Common questions answered.

Are Signals and Dials the same as the Brain?

No. A Signal describes the situation right now. The Brain stores longer-term memory. "Mai is in a meeting now" is a Signal. "Tuesday mornings are usually Mai's busiest" is Brain memory. The Rule Engine reads both.

Can Cognitive Load really be measured?

Not directly. It must be estimated from proxies such as weekday 7am, packed calendar, recent task activity, and user history. The UI should say "estimated."

Is Disclosure Dial the same as privacy settings?

No. Existing privacy settings are often ON/OFF. Disclosure Dial is per domain × per person. Mai can set household finances to Full for AI and her husband, Hidden for children, and Hidden for colleagues.

What do Signals and Dials actually give you?

AI behavior becomes context-sensitive. The system can decide "respond later because Mai is in a meeting" or "avoid bright output because Sota is going to sleep," instead of interrupting at the wrong moment.

The moment is readable. Now connect it to memory, rules, and trust.
