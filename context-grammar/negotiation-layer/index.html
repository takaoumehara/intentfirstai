What you just saw

Interpretation Preview — before acting, the AI shows what it understood. You see the confidence score and the domain it classified your input into. This is the Gate opening negotiation rather than assuming.

Assumption Cards — the AI's premises, made visible and editable. Editable cards can be rephrased. Non-editable cards are hard constraints (e.g., domain sensitivity). Dismissed cards are flagged — the system adjusts.

Confirm vs. Correct — Confirm routes through Stage 6 (Autonomy Resolution) and triggers an AX Pattern response. Correct reopens negotiation: the AI refines its interpretation rather than acting.

Gate firing rules in this demo

Which UI primitive the Gate activates depends on confidence and domain:

  • R34 — Latent awareness → always negotiate as hypothesis
  • R37 — confidence < 0.4 → Assumption Cards
  • R38 — 0.4 ≤ confidence < 0.8 → Interpretation Preview
  • R39 — confidence ≥ 0.8 + low risk + reversible → silent proceed

Phase scope

This demo implements Interpretation Preview, Assumption Cards, and Priority Toggle (Phase 2 — when two values conflict, the system asks which leads for this moment).

The prototype is rule-based. The Decision Schema makes the AI's reasoning explicit and inspectable regardless of whether the underlying model is LLM, heuristic, or scripted.
