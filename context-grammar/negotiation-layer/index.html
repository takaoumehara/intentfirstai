What you just saw

Interpretation Preview shows the AI's current reading of your input before it acts. You see the confidence score and the domain it classified your input into. This is the Gate opening negotiation rather than assuming.

Assumption Cards make the AI's premises visible and editable. Editable cards can be rephrased by clicking on them. Non-editable cards are system constraints (e.g., domain sensitivity cannot be overridden). Dismissed cards are flagged — the system adjusts.

Confirm vs. Correct — Confirm routes through Stage 6 (Autonomy Resolution) and triggers an AX Pattern response. Correct reopens negotiation: the AI refines rather than acts.

Gate firing rules in this demo

Which UI primitive the Gate shows depends on the confidence score and domain:

  • R34 — Latent awareness → always negotiate as hypothesis (never act directly)
  • R37 — confidence < 0.4 → Assumption Cards
  • R38 — 0.4 ≤ confidence < 0.8 → Interpretation Preview
  • R39 — confidence ≥ 0.8 + low risk + reversible → silent (no UI)

Phase 1 scope

This demo implements Interpretation Preview and Assumption Cards. Priority Toggle — when two user values conflict, re-rank in the moment — is Phase 2.

The prototype is rule-based. The Decision Schema makes the AI's reasoning explicit and inspectable regardless of whether the underlying model is LLM, heuristic, or scripted.
