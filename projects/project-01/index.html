Axis 03 · Graduated ArchiveAX · A5Temporal Disclosure↗

The Brain remembers.
And knows what to forget.

Not all memory is equal. The Brain keeps Monday pickup rhythms. It releases the grocery list from three Tuesdays ago. And it locks certain moments — a child's first steps, Kiran's diagnosis — until the right time to surface them again.

Concept · Graduated Archive What gets kept, what gets released, what gets locked until a milestone. The Archive Axis is why the Brain grows more useful over time — not just more full.
